# Privacy policy

SiteBlocker does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no telemetry, analytics, advertising, account system, remote assets, or external service connections.

## Data on your device

The extension stores only the configuration needed to work: sites and their individual blocking modes, one shared weekly schedule, and a settings schema version. These are saved in Chrome's local extension storage, not Chrome sync storage.

When you open the toolbar popup on a website, it can read that tab's URL to offer adding its domain to your blocklist. The URL is processed locally. Only the normalized domain is saved, and only if you choose to add it. The extension does not save browsing history, attempted visits, usage statistics, page content, or logs of your activity.

Chrome itself evaluates the blocking rules. No content scripts inspect the pages you visit. All interface assets are bundled. The extension's content security policy blocks outgoing connections from its extension pages and worker.

## Control and deletion

Remove individual sites and time ranges in settings, or use **Reset all settings** to clear all configuration. Uninstalling removes the extension's local storage and rules through Chrome. Nothing is sent to a developer or third party by this extension. Chrome's own behavior and any device backups are controlled by your browser and operating system.

No privacy settings, tracking opt-outs, or accounts are required.
